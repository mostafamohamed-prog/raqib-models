# RAQIB office characters
Realistic 3D characters for the RAQIB AI Company office.
Source: Microsoft Rocketbox avatar library (MIT licence, see LICENSE-Rocketbox.md), converted to glTF (GLB),
textures resized to 1024 px, animations trimmed. One GLB per head + 6 male and 6 female animations
(sitting, typing, sitting & talking, walking in place, standing idle, talking).
